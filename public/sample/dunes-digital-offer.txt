OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT: JUNIOR SOFTWARE ENGINEER

Following your recent interviews, we are delighted to offer you the position of Junior Software Engineer in our Product Engineering team, reporting to the Engineering Manager. This offer is subject to the terms below and to the issue of your UAE residence visa and work permit.

1. Start date and contract
Your expected start date is 1 March 2027. You will be employed on a fixed-term contract of two (2) years, renewable by mutual agreement.

2. Salary and allowances
Your total monthly salary will be AED 10,000 (ten thousand dirhams), made up as follows:
- Basic salary: AED 4,000 per month
- Housing allowance: AED 4,000 per month
- Transport allowance: AED 1,500 per month
- Other allowance: AED 500 per month
Salary is paid monthly through the Wage Protection System.

3. Probation
You will be on probation for the first nine (9) months of employment. Your full salary will be paid during probation. During probation, either party may end the employment by giving 14 days' written notice.

After probation, either party may end this contract by giving one (1) month's written notice.

5. Working hours
Normal working hours are 8:30 am to 5:30 pm, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. You may be asked to work additional hours to meet project deadlines.

6. Annual leave
You are entitled to twenty-two (22) working days of paid annual leave per year, after completing your probation.

7. Training and certification
The Company will fund your AWS Solutions Architect certification and onboarding bootcamp. If you resign within twenty-four (24) months of your start date, you agree to repay the full cost of this training, estimated at AED 15,000.

8. Documents
For safekeeping, your original passport will be held by the HR department for the duration of your employment and returned on request for travel.

9. Confidentiality and non-competition
You must keep all Company information confidential during and after your employment. For a period of three (3) years after your employment ends, you agree not to work for, consult for, or set up any business that competes with the Company, in any country.

10. Benefits
You will receive medical insurance under the Company's group policy and an annual return air ticket to your home country.
