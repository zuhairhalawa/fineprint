CORAL PHARMACY GROUP LLC
Sharjah

Offer letter — Trainee Pharmacist

Dear Huda,

We are pleased to offer you the position of Trainee Pharmacist on a fixed-term employment contract of two years.

Your gross monthly salary will be AED 9,600, comprising a basic salary of AED 6,000 and a housing allowance of AED 3,600.

Your probation period will be four months, during which you will receive your full salary. During probation the company may terminate your employment by giving 14 days' written notice.

After probation, the notice period is sixty (60) days for both parties.

Your working hours are 8 hours per day, five days a week, on a rotating schedule agreed with your branch manager.

You will be entitled to thirty (30) calendar days of paid annual leave per year.

The company will cover the full cost of your DHA licensing exam and training. You will not be required to repay these costs.

All visa, medical test and Emirates ID costs are borne by the company.

We look forward to welcoming you.

Coral Pharmacy Group — Human Resources
