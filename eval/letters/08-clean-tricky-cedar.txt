Offer letter — Junior QA Engineer

Dear Noor,

We are delighted to offer you the role of Junior QA Engineer on a fixed-term contract of two years.

Salary. AED 8,500 per month: basic salary AED 5,100 and allowances of AED 3,400.

Leave. 30 calendar days of paid annual leave per year.

Training. The company will sponsor your ISTQB certification at no cost to you. There is no repayment obligation.

Documents. Your passport and all personal documents remain in your own possession at all times.

Visa. All visa, work permit and recruitment costs are paid by the company.

Confidentiality. You must keep company information confidential during and after your employment.

We look forward to working with you.
