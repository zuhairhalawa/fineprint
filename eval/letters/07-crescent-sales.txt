CRESCENT MEDIA SALES LLC — SHARJAH

Appointment Letter: Junior Account Executive

Dear Candidate,

We are pleased to appoint you as Junior Account Executive under a two-year fixed-term contract.

Your monthly salary will be AED 6,000 (basic AED 3,600, allowances AED 2,400).

Your probation period is 6 months with full pay. The company may end your employment during probation with 14 days' written notice. After probation, the notice period is one month.

Your normal working hours are 48 hours per week across six days of 8 hours.

You are entitled to 30 days' annual leave per year.

The cost of your employment visa, Emirates ID and medical test (AED 3,500) will be deducted from your salary in equal instalments over your first three months.

Your passport will be kept by the company for safekeeping and released for travel upon approval.

Welcome aboard.

Crescent Media Sales LLC
